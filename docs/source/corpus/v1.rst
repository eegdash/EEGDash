#################
EEGDash Corpus v1
#################

Version 1 extends version 0 to every publicly usable dataset named by REVE's paper:
|v1_datasets| datasets, of which |v1_to_convert| are ingested by EEGDash and wait for
conversion and |to_ingest| is still to be ingested. The numbers of the added datasets
come from EEGDash's records until they are converted; the views below are those of v0,
with the added datasets stacked by status.

What v1 adds
============

.. corpus-figure:: v1-adds-table

SparrKULee carries CC BY-NC 4.0 in NEMAR's dataset description (EEGDash's record lists
none), so a v1 Zarr store that includes it carries non-commercial terms; its EEGDash
records carry no signal metadata, so its hours are measured at conversion. Siena Scalp
EEG is held and waits for its deposit on NEMAR; its numbers follow.

Electrode coverage
==================

One mark per electrode position (|v1_positions| positions in |v1_buckets| channel
sets): v0's positions measured in the Zarr store, plus the channel sets of
|v1_mapped| of the datasets v1 adds, read from EEGDash's records with the hours those
records state; hover a mark for the v0 and added shares. Not on the map yet:
|v1_unmapped| (no channel metadata in EEGDash's records, or not yet ingested).
