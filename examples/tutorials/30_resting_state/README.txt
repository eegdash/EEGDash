Resting-state condition decoding
================================

Use actual HBN instruction markers to label eyes-open and eyes-closed
intervals. Compute alpha-band power, fit scaling on training subjects only,
and evaluate each held-out participant once. Plots show the measured spectra
and scores without asserting a textbook effect or a minimum accuracy.

``plot_30_eyes_open_closed.py`` uses three explicit HBN recordings, about
281 MB total. Retain EEGDASH_CACHE_DIR across runs. This task-specific example
is larger than the small SSVEP examples in the introductory track.
