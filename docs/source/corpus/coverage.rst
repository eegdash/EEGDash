########
Coverage
########

These pages track every dataset REVE's paper names (|paper_named|) and every entry its
release card lists (|card_entries|, |card_only| of them not in the paper): |universe|
datasets. |v0_datasets| are converted and released in EEGDash Corpus v0. Of the rest,
|not_redistributable| cannot be redistributed: |blocked| are blocked by their licences
(|blocked_nd| No Derivatives, |blocked_none| with no licence stated) and |gated| are
gated by access agreements that forbid redistribution (|gated_names|). |excluded| are
excluded for other reasons: |excluded_prose|. The data on this page is as of |synced|.

How to follow or contribute
===========================

The corpus is maintained in the open on the `EEGDash issue tracker
<https://github.com/eegdash/EEGDash/issues>`_: propose a public dataset for a later
version, or report a licence or a number that looks wrong.
