#################
EEGDash Corpus v0
#################

Version 0 is REVE's public release rebuilt from the archives and converted: every
number on this page is measured in the converted Zarr store, as of |synced|.

==========

.. corpus-figure:: v0-kpis

Electrode coverage
==================

One mark per electrode position of the Zarr store (|v0_positions| positions in
|v0_buckets| channel sets), coloured and sized by the hours recorded there or by the
number of datasets that include it.

Recordings

Recording length
----------------

How long one recording lasts in each dataset, summarised by its quantiles (the
per-recording lengths themselves are not shipped), on a log axis from minutes to days.

.. corpus-figure:: lengths

Recordings per subject
----------------------

How much each subject contributes: recordings and hours per subject, the median subject
and the subject with the most.

.. corpus-figure:: per-subject

People
======

Ages and sex of the subjects, for the datasets whose records state them.

.. corpus-figure:: people

From archive to status
======================

Where the hours come from and under which licence: archive, then licence, then status;
switch the flow width to datasets to see the datasets without hours.

.. corpus-figure:: flow-licence

Training budget
===============

What one training epoch reads: tokens per dataset, grouped by paradigm, each tile
shaded by its share of the tokens.

.. corpus-figure:: tokens

Montages
========

A channel is one electrode. The columns are the datasets, by channel count, and the
rows the |montage_rows| standard names used by at least |montage_min| datasets, so nets
with their own naming, such as EGI or BioSemi, show only the electrodes whose names
coincide.
